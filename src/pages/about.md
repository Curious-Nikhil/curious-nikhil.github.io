# Namaste! 🙏

I have always been a space enthusiast from the start. Loved star wars and the space shuttle. Hardly relatable but for me it was everything. Growing up I discovered new things like astronomy and SpaceX. To me, the sky was filled with infinite things waiting to be discovered for the first time. I was emotional when I watched Cosmos: A space-time odyssey!

What began with _Star Wars_, shuttle launches, and late-night skywatching turned into real projects: model rockets, balloon missions, simulations, and embedded systems.

I am learning new things every day and am so glad to be part of the space sciences community. From watching NatGeo documentaries about Space missions as a kid after school to now building spacecraft with brilliant engineers at Dhruva Space, life has come full circle. It's humbling and exhilarating to turn deep-tech ideas into flight hardware, right here in the city I grew up. Designed and built in India, powering Space applications across the world! 🇮🇳

This site is my lab notebook on the internet: experiments, project breakdowns, lessons learned, and occasional failures that taught me more than success ever did.

I’m here to keep learning, keep shipping, and keep looking up . If you’re into space, science, and building ambitious things from scratch, you’ll feel at home here. Feel free to DM over on my socials!
